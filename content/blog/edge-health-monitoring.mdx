## Why edge?

Edge processing can reduce latency and keep systems resilient when connectivity is limited.

## Common pitfalls

- Model updates and versioning
- Debugging on-device behavior
- Observability gaps

## A practical approach

Start with a minimal edge workload, define clear fallbacks, and keep the cloud path as a safety net.
