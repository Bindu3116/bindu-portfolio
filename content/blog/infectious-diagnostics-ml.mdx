## Problem framing

For public health screening, false negatives can be costly. That pushed our team toward **recall-focused** optimization with careful threshold selection.

## What helped most

- Feature engineering from clinical context (e.g., comorbidity counts)
- Strong baselines + simple model comparisons
- Proper split strategy (avoid leakage)

## Metrics that mattered

- ROC-AUC for ranking quality
- Recall to reduce missed positives
- Precision trade-offs based on screening capacity
