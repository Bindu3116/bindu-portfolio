## Why EEG + ECG?

EEG and ECG capture different physiological views of stress. The key is to align **timing**, handle **noise**, and learn representations that generalize across subjects.

## Pipeline sketch

- Signal cleaning and segmentation
- Feature learning with encoders (CNN / Transformer)
- Fusion (early, late, or attention-based)
- Evaluation: subject-wise splits, calibration, robustness checks
